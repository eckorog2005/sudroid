# Sudroid privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Sudroid does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

- The app has no internet permission, no ads, no analytics and no accounts.
- Your saved games, settings and statistics are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
- If Android's backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your device backup with Google. That backup is handled by Android under Google's privacy policy; Sudroid never sees it.
- Uninstalling the app deletes its data from your device.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/eckorog2005/sudroid/issues.
