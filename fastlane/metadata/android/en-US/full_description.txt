Sudroid is a clean, classic Sudoku game. No ads, no accounts, no tracking: just puzzles.

DAILY CHALLENGE
A new puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Solve it daily to build a streak.

900 PUZZLES, THREE LEVELS
Easy, Medium and Hard. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and the newer puzzles are graded by the techniques you need to solve them.

HINTS THAT TEACH
Stuck? A hint fills in the next cell and tells you why: a naked single, a hidden single, or locked candidates.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Pencil marks that clear themselves when you place a digit
• Unlimited undo and redo
• Highlights for the selected row, column, box and matching digits
• A number pad that counts how many of each digit are left
• Show mistakes immediately, only when you check, or never
• Pause hides the board and stops the clock

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Games completed, best and average times for each difficulty, and day streaks.

MADE FOR ANY SCREEN
Material You colours, dark mode, landscape, tablet and foldable layouts, TalkBack support, and text that follows your font size.
